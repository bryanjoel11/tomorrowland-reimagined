The proposal

Six upgrades for Tomorrowland

Each one starts from something I watched go wrong on shift. Every card lists the problem, the fix, and what I expect it to move: throughput, wait accuracy, guest flow or per-cap spend. Items tagged [verify] are working assumptions I still need to confirm.

    About the project

    Why I rebuilt Tomorrowland

    I worked attractions in Tomorrowland at Magic Kingdom. Every shift I watched the same things go wrong in the same places: exits jamming up, queue merges spilling onto the walkway, mobile-order guests parked in the middle of the promenade.

    This project is the land the way I would run it. It is a 3D model laid out to match the real Tomorrowland, from the hub bridge and Rocket Tower Plaza to Space Mountain, TRON and the Speedway, with six changes I would make from the operator's side. Flip between Today and Upgraded to see each change in place, and watch a simple guest-flow model react.

    What it shows

    Operations insight turned into a proposal. Each upgrade names the guest or operator pain, the intervention, and the metric it should move. Two are revenue plays (an add-on window at mobile-order pickup, a cart at a high-volume exit); the rest protect capacity, which is revenue too.

    How the guest model works

    750 guests walk a graph of the land's walkways, picking weighted next stops. Walking speed falls as a stretch gets crowded. In Upgraded, the pinch points widen, standing crowds move off the path, and guests use live wait info to route around hot spots. It is illustrative, not calibrated to park data.

    How it is built

    Vite, TypeScript and three.js. Every building, ride and path is generated in code: no downloaded models, logos or official artwork. Instancing keeps the guests, track and trees fast, and the site is fully static.

    What is next

    The current six pins are placeholders positioned on the real layout. Next I am replacing them with my own list from the floor, confirming the [verify] items, and tightening the layout details I flagged in the project notes.
