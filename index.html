Fan concept / operations case study

Tomorrowland Reimagined

I worked attractions in Tomorrowland at Magic Kingdom. Every shift I watched the same things go wrong in the same places: the coaster exit jamming up, the queue merge spilling onto the walkway, mobile-order guests parked in the middle of the promenade.

This is the land the way I would run it. It is a stylized model built entirely in code. Flip between Today and Upgraded, open the six pins to see each problem, the fix and what I expect it to move, and watch the guest dots spread out when the pinch points open up.

  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan
  • Pins open the Problem / Fix / Impact cards

Unofficial fan concept and personal case study. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Disney. No official artwork, logos or models are used; attraction names appear only as references for where the ideas came from. Numbers are illustrative.
